Projects

YOLOv8 on AWS ECS/Fargate: Inference Serving at Scale ↗

Compared scalable ECS/Fargate deployment against a single EC2 baseline for YOLOv8 object detection. Ran 5,000 COCO 2017 images through quiet, sustained, and burst traffic patterns while measuring p50/p95/p99 latency and CPU utilization in CloudWatch.

YOLOv8

TabDDPM: Can Diffusion Models Fix Clinical Class Imbalance? ↗
In progress

Hospital readmission dataset with an 89/11 class split across 101,766 patients. Comparing TabDDPM, SMOTE, and CTGAN for generating synthetic minority-class records, with XGBoost evaluated on F1 for the rare class and AUC-ROC.

TabDDPM XGBoost
I built the class-conditioning component so the model generates specifically readmitted-class patients, injected the label into training, and wrote the generation pipeline from noise to synthetic patient records.
What Actually Drives RNN Performance? A 36-Config Study ↗

Tested 36 configurations of RNN, LSTM, and BiLSTM on IMDB sentiment (50K reviews). Varied optimizer, activation function, sequence length, and gradient clipping. Key finding: switching Adam to SGD hurt more than switching a vanilla RNN to a BiLSTM helped. Best config: 81.6% accuracy.

NLTKLSTMBiLSTM
Semantic Search Engine with BPE Tokenization ↗

Built from scratch over Project Gutenberg texts. BPE handles subword tokenization, edit-distance autocorrect handles noisy queries, and TF-IDF cosine similarity handles ranking. Searching "luve" still surfaces results about love. Index is cached after the first build.

BPETF-IDFEdit Distance
N-Gram Language Modeling on Penn Treebank ↗

Implemented unigram through 4-gram models and compared Laplace smoothing, linear interpolation, and Stupid Backoff by perplexity on PTB. Outputs a comparison chart and generates sample sentences from the best model so the qualitative difference is visible, not just numeric.

N-grams
Does Dimensionality Reduction Help Classify MNIST? ↗

Compared PCA, Kernel PCA (Nystrom approximation), LLE, and raw 784-pixel vectors before kNN classification. PCA compressed inputs to 44 dimensions and beat raw pixels: 97.23% vs 96.91%. Tracks accuracy and runtime together so the tradeoffs are explicit.

PCAKernel PCALLEkNN
Ocular Disease Recognition from Retinal Fundus Images ↗

Used the ODIR-5K dataset (10,000+ retinal fundus images with patient demographics) for multi-class ocular disease classification, covering EDA, image preprocessing, and CNN training.

EDACNN
I handled the EDA: disease prevalence distributions, demographic breakdowns, and statistical tests on correlations between patient characteristics and disease labels. Also wrote the analysis section of the final report.

Background

I spent five years at Cognizant before starting my MS, working on financial data systems for stock exchange clients in India. The stakes were real: a buggy model affected index rebalancing decisions, a slow validation tool meant eight-hour reconciliation cycles. That context shaped how I think about this work.

During those years I built dashboards that clients actually checked before each monthly release, trained classifiers that cut a 25-hour bug validation process to 4 minutes per 100 test cases, and designed a migration tool that turned an 8-hour reconciliation into a 5-minute one. I also presented findings to clients directly each month, which taught me as much about communicating data as analyzing it.

My internship in summer 2025 brought a different kind of problem: building a salary intelligence platform from 20M+ records across 15+ sources, with anomaly detection and ETL pipelines in Spark and ClickHouse. Good reminder that data engineering and data science are usually the same job.

Outside of work I am curious about generative models for structured data, classical NLP foundations, and how production ML systems actually behave under load.
